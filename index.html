Our Services

Counsel across the full spectrum of Zambian commercial and civil law.

Conveyancing

Assignments, transfers of properties, and creation of debentures, mortgages and guarantees on behalf of financial institutions and individual clients.

Dispute Resolution

Settlement of disputes before Courts of Law and Arbitral Tribunals. Civil, commercial and labour litigation including debt collection and foreclosures.

Legal Drafting

Debentures, mortgages, commercial agreements, service agreements, trusts, powers of attorney, tenancy and lease agreements, and deeds of assignment.

Advisory Services

Research and opinions across Banking Law, Company Law, Commercial Law, Employment and Labour Relations, Land Law, Intellectual Property and Tort.

Company Secretarial

Company formation and incorporation, filing of annual returns, registration of company resolutions, and preparation of securities.

A Standard of Excellence

Every client. Every matter. The same measure of decorum, professionalism, and etiquette — regardless of background or resources.

Turnaround Time

Our standard turnaround time for actionable items is 2 business days from receipt of instructions. We remain flexible to agree shorter periods where circumstances demand.

Periodic Updates

We provide proactive updates on all matters under our attention. Clients need not chase us — we come to you. Interim updates are also available on request.

Confidentiality

Attorney-client privilege is the backbone of the legal profession. We uphold the highest standards of confidentiality on every matter, at all times, subject only to a lawful court order.

Transparent Billing

Fees are charged in accordance with Statutory Instruments. Itemised bills are provided on request. All bills are to be settled or disputed within 21 days of presentation.
